# Third-party MLB team logos

Team marks, names, and logos are the property of Major League Baseball and
their respective clubs. These assets are vendored here **for personal,
non-commercial dial use only** — they are shown as small next-game hero
images on a personal desktop display and are not redistributed, sold, or
used for any commercial purpose.

Source: fetched at build time from the ESPN CDN
(`https://a.espncdn.com/i/teamlogos/mlb/500/{abbr}.png`) via
`support/fetch_mlb_logos.py`, then resized to 56×56 with transparency
preserved by `support/gen_mlb_logos.py`.

This project has no affiliation with, and is not endorsed or sponsored by,
MLB, any MLB club, or ESPN.
